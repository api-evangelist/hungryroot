# Hungryroot

> Hungryroot is a personalized grocery and meal-planning service that blends meal-kit convenience with grocery-store variety. An AI personalization engine builds each customer's cart from their dietary preferences, health goals, and budget, drawing on 50,000+ recipes and curated grocery products across high-protein, gluten-free, vegan, and other diets. Hungryroot is a direct-to-consumer company (backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners) with a consumer web and mobile experience; it does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI, SDKs, well-known catalog, security.txt, or llms.txt as of this pipeline pass.
